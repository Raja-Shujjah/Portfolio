Selected Work

Case studies

Real systems, framed by the problem they solved and the outcome they produced.

CASE 01
Lead Sourcing

Google Maps Lead Scraper System

Problem

A sales team built prospect lists by hand from Google Maps — hours of copy-pasting business names, phone numbers, and addresses, with duplicates and stale entries.

Solution

An n8n workflow that searches Maps by location and category, extracts and de-duplicates every result, validates contact details, and writes clean records straight to a shared sheet.

8 hrs → 15 minWeekly list-building time, fully automated and ready for outreach.
CASE 02
Pipeline Ops

Automated CRM Pipeline

Problem

Inbound leads sat untouched for hours. Reps weren't assigned automatically and follow-ups depended on someone remembering to send them.

Solution

A webhook pipeline that captures every new lead, scores and routes it to the right rep instantly, opens a deal at the correct stage, and fires a timed follow-up sequence.

35% fasterFirst-response time, with zero leads left unassigned.
CASE 03
Data Extraction

Business Data Extraction Workflow

Problem

Market research relied on manually gathering company data from multiple sites — slow, inconsistent, and impossible to keep current.

Solution

A scheduled extraction system that pulls structured data via APIs and scraping, normalizes it into one format, and refreshes the dataset automatically on a recurring run.

1,000+ / moRecords collected and updated with no manual intervention.
